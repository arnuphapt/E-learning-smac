THESIS: a student practice environment that relieves nursing exam anxiety through soft tactile claymorphism while maintaining rigorous study density, high legibility, and effortless keyboard/touch operation. Refuses harsh flat tables, aggressive high-volume cartoonish bloat, and inaccessible low-contrast pastels.
OWN-WORLD: Soft Clay system: soothing porcelain ground (#f3f6f9), cushioned card volumes (border-radius 20px, dual soft inset highlights inset 2px 2px 4px rgba(255,255,255,.9) + inset -2px -2px 4px rgba(13,110,140,.06) + soft diffuse drop shadow 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15,23,42,.07)), tactile button keycaps with satisfying pressed states, pillowy badges, and crisp high-contrast typography (body contrast > 5:1, deep navy #0f172a on white clay surfaces).
STORY: the student enters looking for practice, feels an inviting, tactile calm, taps into a set and lesson with immediate visual feedback, works through exam questions with tactile choice buttons that feel physically selected, and reviews results with clear topic mastery meters.
